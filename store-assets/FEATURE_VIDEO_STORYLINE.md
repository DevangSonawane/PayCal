# Tabbb — 20-Second Feature Video Storyline

**Purpose:** Explain Tabbb in one quick, relatable story: shared spending stays clear, fair, and easy to settle.

**Audience:** Flatmates, friends on trips, couples, and any group that shares expenses.

## YouTube upload copy

**Title**

Tabbb: Split Bills, Keep Friendships | Shared Expense App

**Description**

Someone pays for dinner. Someone else gets the cab. Then comes the awkward part: “Who owes what?”

Tabbb keeps shared expenses simple. Create a shared tab for your flat, trip, couple, or crew; add an expense, choose who is involved, and let Tabbb split it fairly. When it’s time to settle, Tabbb works out the fewest payments needed to square up.

Split bills. Keep friendships.

## Edit notes

- Start with a recognisable social moment, then move quickly into the product.
- Use one continuous example group and the same amounts throughout so the story feels real.
- Keep the UI footage large and legible; the app’s split and settle-up states are the proof points.
- End on the logo and final line for at least one second.
